Bring research to your school.

A {{ brand }} Chapter is a student-run research org/club at your school. Whether you already have a different club under a different name or don't have a club established yet, we'd love to help other high schoolers make research more accessible. Reach out to us to get access to our curriculum, slideshows, and organization; resources, example papers, and guest speakers; and collaboration between our global chapters network and journal. Fill out the form below or email us, and one of our outreach coordinators will give you access to the above!

What your chapter gets

You're not starting from scratch

A finalized curriculum

Meeting-by-meeting slide decks, worksheets, and resources, with objectives set for each period so leaders can focus on running the club, not inventing it.

Shared guest speakers

Chapters across the network share access to guest speakers (researchers, professors, and college students) scheduled through the year.

Peer editing across schools

Your members swap drafts with students at other chapters for feedback beyond your own club room.

A path to publication

Finished papers are eligible for the {{ brand }} Journal, plus guidance on competitions and journals organized by deadline and topic.
